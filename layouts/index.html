Who I am

Twenty years building technology

Seven years at G-Log designing the technical and systems architecture behind what became Oracle Transportation Management, and leading its datacenter deployments. Co-founded MavenWire in 2006 and led it for a decade, bootstrapped, to $14M a year in revenue and 150 people, with global offices; sold in 2016.

A decade in healing and facilitation work

After the sale I stepped away from tech. I facilitated healing for combat veterans with PTSD and people with complex trauma, co-founded AdventureX (transformational expeditions in remote places), and did the inner work myself. What I learned there: how to hold people in their hardest places without flinching, and that the inner work and the outer work are one.

Back to technology, since 2024

Node0, a sovereign infrastructure site, built and operated with a set of agents; Standpoint Labs; and daily, genuine collaboration with digital intelligence as partners rather than tools. That work informs how I advise founders today: I recommend what I run.
